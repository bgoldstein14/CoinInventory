Print a report

Each of these opens your browser’s print window. Choose “Save as PDF” there to keep a copy as a file, or send it straight to a printer.

Insurance & Appraisal Schedule

A dated list of the coins you actually own, with the details that identify each one, its grade and certification, what you paid, what it is worth — and photographs. This is the document to give an insurer or an appraiser.

  • {{ insurableCount() }} coins (want-list, on-order and sold coins are left out, and the page says so)
  • Your private notes are not included
  • Covers your whole collection, not just what is filtered on screen

Full Inventory Listing

Everything currently showing in the table, printed in the same order and through the same columns. A reference copy for your own files, or for anyone who needs to read the collection on paper.

  • {{ listingCount() }} coins — whatever your filters and sort are set to right now
  • The filter and sort in use are printed on the page
  • No photographs, so it is quick to produce and quick to read

Export as a spreadsheet file

Comma-separated files, for opening in Excel or loading into another program. These cannot carry photographs.

Full CSV

Every coin and every field, in a file this app can read back in. Use this to move or back up the collection.

Insurance CSV

The same coins as the printed schedule, as a spreadsheet. Useful if an insurer asks for data rather than a document.
