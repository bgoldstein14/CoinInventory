{{ soldRuleSentence }} A Quicken file has no such column, so a coin is taken to be sold when its buys and sells net to nothing — and the sale's date and amount are then imported with it.

Quicken records what you paid and when, and the sale if there was one, so Cost, Purchase Date, Sold Price and Sold Date come across. It does not record who you bought from or sold to, so Purchased From and Sold To are left empty rather than filled with the Quicken account name — which you would only have to clear again. A CSV or Excel file can map those columns if you have them.
